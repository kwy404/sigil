# SIGIL brand

SIGIL is a personal companion for Android and iOS that pilots the phone with signs drawn in the air, seen by the front camera, and with voice. Everything it sees and hears is processed on the device.

**The concept in one line:** you draw a sign in the air and the phone obeys.

## 1. The name

A sigil is a drawn sign that carries power. The word comes from the Latin *sigillum*, a small sign or seal, the diminutive of *signum*. That is exactly what a gesture is in this product: a small sign, drawn by a hand, that the phone treats as a command. Every gesture is a sigil. The app is the place where they are read.

Why SIGIL works:

- **It names the interaction, not the technology.** No "AI", no "gesture", no "air". The name describes what the person does: they draw a sign.
- **It carries privacy in Portuguese.** *Sigilo* means confidentiality, and *sigiloso* means confidential. For Portuguese speakers the name quietly says "kept secret", which is the truth of an app that never sends a camera frame or a voice clip anywhere.
- **It is a seal.** A sigillum sealed letters. SIGIL keeps what it sees sealed inside the phone.
- **It is short and speakable.** Two syllables, five letters, the same spelling in every market.
- **It does not sound like a person.** SIGIL is a companion, not a character. There is no mascot, no face and no human name.

Write the name as **SIGIL** in the logo and in headlines set in capitals. In running text write **Sigil** (one capital), never "sigil app" or "SIGIL AI".

## 2. The wake word

The wake word is **"Sigil"**.

| | |
|---|---|
| English | /ˈsɪdʒ.ɪl/, "SIJ-il", stress on the first syllable |
| Portuguese (Brazil) | /ˈsi.ʒiw/, "SÍ-jiu", the final L softens to a U sound |
| Detector | on device keyword spotter trained on both pronunciations |

Rules:

- **The wake word is never required.** An open palm summons the Control Sphere in silence. Voice is a second door, not the main one.
- **Address, then speak.** "Sigil, read my last message." After the sphere opens, follow ups need no wake word for 8 seconds.
- **Stop words always work.** "Sigil, stop", "Sigil, never mind" and "Sigil, close" cancel at any point, even in the middle of an action chain.
- **It is a rare word.** Two syllables with an unusual onset keep false wakes low in everyday English and Portuguese speech.
- **Nothing leaves the phone.** The spotter runs on the device, and the audio buffer before the wake word is discarded continuously.

## 4. Voice and tone

SIGIL speaks like a calm, precise colleague who happens to be funny once in a while. It never performs. It is the quiet heads up display of a pilot, not a game show host.

### Principles

1. **Calm.** Short sentences. No exclamation marks. Never urgent unless something is actually urgent.
2. **Precise.** Say what happened, with the real number. "Sent to Ana." Not "Your message was sent successfully".
3. **Dry humor, rarely.** At most one light line in a long session, never during an error that cost the person something, never at their expense.
4. **Verbs first.** Buttons and hints start with the action: "Pinch to confirm", "Show me your hand".
5. **Honest about limits.** If SIGIL cannot see or cannot do something, it says so plainly and offers the next best path.
6. **Private by default, and says so.** When it matters, mention that something stayed on the phone. Do not repeat it on every screen.

### Mechanics

- No emoji, no exclamation marks, no "Oops", no "Uh oh", no "Awesome".
- Numbers as digits: 3 apps, 18 ms, 2 steps.
- One idea per message. Two lines maximum in the HUD.
- First person ("I") is allowed but used sparingly. Prefer describing the state: "Listening." instead of "I am listening to you now."
- Same tone in both languages. Portuguese copy uses "você" and the same brevity, never a literal translation of English idioms.

### Examples

| Moment | English | Portuguese (Brazil) |
|---|---|---|
| Hand left the camera | Show me your hand. | Mostre a mão. |
| Too dark to track | Too dark for me to see your hand. | Está escuro demais para ver sua mão. |
| Listening | Listening. | Ouvindo. |
| Thinking for more than 1.2 s | Working on it. | Resolvendo. |
| Confirm before sending | Send to Ana? Pinch to confirm. | Enviar para Ana? Faça a pinça para confirmar. |
| Done | Sent. | Enviado. |
| Gesture not recognized | Missed that one. Try a little slower. | Não peguei. Tente um pouco mais devagar. |
| Third miss in a row (dry) | We can also just talk. | A gente também pode só conversar. |
| Battery saver on (dry) | Particles off. Battery on. | Partículas desligadas. Bateria ligada. |
| Camera paused for privacy | Camera off. Voice still works. | Câmera desligada. A voz continua. |
| Nothing was sent | Processed on this phone. Nothing left it. | Processado neste celular. Nada saiu dele. |

Avoid: "Hey there!", "Oops, something went wrong", "I'm so sorry about that", "Your AI assistant is thinking...", any copy that pretends SIGIL has feelings.

## 5. The logo

### The mark

The mark is an **S drawn in two strokes around a point of light**. The spine of the S is not drawn: the light is the spine.

- **Read it as a letter:** the S of SIGIL.
- **Read it as a gesture:** two strokes of a fingertip closing on a point, like a thumb and an index finger meeting in a pinch.
- **Read it as the product:** the point is the holographic cursor and the core of the Control Sphere. The two arcs orbit it; they are the same arcs the sphere uses when it thinks.
- **Read it as a seal:** the mark has half turn symmetry. Turn it upside down and it is the same sign, the way a sigil or a stamp should be.

The mark is built on the same geometry as the icon set (glyphfox rules): a 24 by 24 grid, 2 px stroke, round caps, 2 px padding, no transforms.

| Part | Geometry on the 24 grid |
|---|---|
| Top bowl | arc of radius 4.7 centered at (12, 7.3), from -32° to 152°, drawn counterclockwise |
| Bottom bowl | the top bowl turned half a turn around (12, 12): radius 4.7 centered at (12, 16.7), from 148° to -28° |
| Point of light | circle of radius 1 at (12, 12), which renders as a 4 px disc with the 2 px stroke |
| Gap | 1.8 px between each inner arc end and the point, so the light always reads as free |

The arcs are drawn toward the light. In the luminous version each arc fades in from its outer end and is brightest where it meets the point, like a light trail ending at a fingertip.

### The wordmark

SIGIL is set in a custom monoline wordmark, not a font. It shares the logic of the mark: a two circle S (the mark with its spine drawn in), a geometric G with a flat bar, and straight I and L. Cap height 20 units, stroke 1.8 units, round ends, wide tracking for a calm heads up display rhythm. Never retype the wordmark in Sora or any other font.

### Lockups and clear space

- **Horizontal lockup:** mark on the left, wordmark cap height at 48 percent of the drawn mark height, gap of 20 percent of the drawn mark height, wordmark centered on the point of light.
- **Clear space:** one bowl radius of the mark (4.7 grid units, about 22 percent of the drawn mark height) on every side.
- **Minimum sizes:** mark 16 px, wordmark 56 px wide, lockup 120 px wide.

### Color

| Background | Mark | Wordmark |
|---|---|---|
| Dark (default) | luminous: aqua arcs fading to white, white point with an aqua halo; or flat: `#E8EEF6` arcs, `#5EF2DF` point | `#E8EEF6` |
| Light | `#0A0F1C` arcs, `#0E968C` point | `#0A0F1C` |
| One color | everything in one color, any brand neutral | same color |

### Do not

- Do not close the S, draw its spine, or put the point inside a bowl.
- Do not rotate the mark (half turn excepted, which changes nothing), outline it, add bevels or drop shadows.
- Do not put the mark in a circle badge or a speech bubble.
- Do not use a color outside the lumen family for the point.
- Do not add faces, eyes, limbs or any character to the mark. There is no mascot.

### The mark in motion

| State | Motion |
|---|---|
| Idle | the arcs breathe with the sphere, 5.2 s per breath, scale 0.985 to 1.015 |
| Listening | the arcs open by 8° each and pulse with the voice envelope |
| Thinking | the arcs orbit the point, one turn every 1.6 s, linear |
| Done | the point brightens once to 1.25 and settles, 320 ms |
| Reduced motion | static mark, state shown by the label only |

### The app icon

A dark tile (`#0F1E36` at the center falling to `#020409` at the corners) with a faint glass sphere and the luminous mark inside it, plus a few bioluminescent motes. On Android the sphere lives in the background layer and the mark in the foreground layer, so launchers that move the layers show real parallax. The themed (monochrome) icon is the flat mark in white.

## 7. Wiring notes for app.json

The image files are in place. Three values in `app.json` still carry the Expo template and should change so the new art shows correctly:

1. `expo-splash-screen` plugin, `backgroundColor`: `#208AEF` to `#070B15` (the luminous splash mark is designed for the dark base).
2. `android.adaptiveIcon.backgroundColor`: `#E6F4FE` to `#070B15` (used only where the background image is not).
3. `ios.icon` points to `./assets/expo.icon`, which still holds the Expo symbol. Copy `design/logo/expo.icon/` over `assets/expo.icon/`, or point `ios.icon` to `./assets/images/icon.png`.
